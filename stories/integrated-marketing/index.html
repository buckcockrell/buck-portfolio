Story 05

How do you help a large organization tell one cohesive story?

Connecting the Dots Across Integrated Marketing

Helped strengthen Microsoft Advertising's marketing strategy by connecting product marketing, sales, digital, events, communications, and partner teams around a more integrated go-to-market approach.

01

The challenge

Great work was happening across the organization, but it wasn't always happening together.

As Microsoft Advertising continued to evolve, one challenge became increasingly clear: great work was happening across the organization, but it wasn't always happening together. Product launches, campaigns, events, web experiences, seller communications, and partner marketing often operated on different timelines with different priorities. The opportunity wasn't to create more marketing. It was to create better alignment, ensuring every customer touchpoint reinforced a consistent story and supported broader business objectives.

Integrated marketing isn't about managing more channels. It's about helping organizations tell one cohesive story, regardless of where customers encounter it.

02

The approach

Bring teams together earlier and more intentionally.

Working across product marketing, field, digital, communications, creative, and leadership teams, I helped establish integrated marketing processes that brought those organizations together earlier and more intentionally.

Rather than treating launches as a series of independent deliverables, we built coordinated marketing plans that aligned messaging, digital experiences, executive communications, events, social, and seller readiness around a shared strategy. The result was a more connected marketing organization that delivered greater consistency for customers while giving regional and customer-facing teams the flexibility to tailor campaigns without losing strategic alignment.

The business result

More coordinated execution across Microsoft's advertising organization.

Helped establish integrated marketing processes that aligned product marketing, digital, communications, events, sales, and partner teams around coordinated go-to-market execution, highlighting the strength of our consumer brands, precision of our targeting, and performance of our ad units.

03

What I learned
