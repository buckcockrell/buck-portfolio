Story 03 Microsoft

When critical work can't pause, who does an organization trust to keep it moving?

Trusted to Lead

Twice asked to step into Senior Product Marketing Manager responsibilities during organizational transitions while continuing to support my existing Microsoft Teams portfolio.

Company Microsoft
Focus Leadership continuity
Capabilities
  • Senior Product Marketing
  • Cross-functional Leadership
  • Executive Collaboration
  • Product Strategy
  • Business Continuity
  • Organizational Agility
01

The challenge

Leadership transitions can't become business interruptions.

During my time supporting Microsoft Teams, I was twice asked to step into Senior Product Marketing Manager responsibilities during periods of organizational transition. The expectation wasn't simply to maintain the status quo. It was to provide continuity for the business while continuing to deliver against my existing responsibilities.

Each assignment required rapidly building context across new product areas, establishing trusted relationships with engineering and product teams, and ensuring active initiatives continued moving forward without disrupting existing workstreams.

The best measure of readiness isn't the title you hold. It's the work people trust you to do before you have it.

02

The approach

Create confidence while keeping momentum.

Whether developing product positioning, supporting go-to-market planning, partnering with engineering, or enabling the field, my focus remained consistent: keep the business moving forward while giving teams confidence that priorities wouldn't slip during the transition.

Rather than treating these assignments as temporary coverage, I approached them as opportunities to establish alignment quickly, remove uncertainty, and ensure customers and internal stakeholders experienced continuity instead of disruption.

The business result

Leadership continuity across two critical Microsoft product portfolios.

2 Senior product marketing assignments
2 Teams portfolios supported simultaneously

Provided product marketing leadership across Teams Phone and Teams Chat & Collaboration while continuing responsibility for my existing Microsoft Teams portfolio, helping maintain momentum during two organizational transitions.

03

What I learned

Trust is earned long before a title changes.

These experiences reinforced that leadership is measured less by title than by trust. Being asked to step into these roles on multiple occasions reflected the organization's confidence in my ability to adapt quickly, lead through ambiguity, and deliver consistent results across multiple product portfolios.

Leadership isn't conferred by title. It's earned through trust, consistency, and the confidence others place in your ability to move the business forward.
