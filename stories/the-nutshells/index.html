Story 10

How do you help professionals show who they are beyond the limits of a traditional résumé?

Reimagining the Professional Profile

Created The Nutshells, a digital storytelling platform designed to help professionals showcase who they are beyond the limits of a traditional résumé.

Company The Nutshells
Focus Product strategy and entrepreneurship
Capabilities

  • Entrepreneurship

01

The challenge

Traditional résumés summarize careers, but they rarely reveal the person behind the credentials.

For decades, résumés have served as the standard way to present professional experience. While effective for summarizing careers, they often struggle to communicate the qualities that matter most: how someone thinks, solves problems, leads teams, and creates value.

I saw an opportunity to rethink the professional profile, creating a richer, more engaging way for people to tell their stories.

Great product ideas require more than conviction. They require the discipline to validate assumptions, learn from the market, and recognize when the timing or investment isn't yet right.

02

The approach

Build a richer and more human way to tell a professional story.

I conceived and developed The Nutshells as a platform that combined concise written narratives, video, and visual storytelling into a digital profile that better reflected an individual's experience, personality, and accomplishments.

Rather than replacing the résumé, the goal was to complement it by giving hiring managers and professional networks a more authentic understanding of the person behind the credentials. Every aspect of the experience, from the content structure to the user journey, was designed to make professional storytelling more engaging, memorable, and human.

Building the platform required balancing product strategy, user experience, branding, content design, and technology while continually refining the concept through customer feedback and iteration. As both the product creator and strategist, I was responsible for transforming an initial idea into a functioning product that people could use to tell their stories in a new way.

The business result

A functioning product, early adoption, and valuable market insight.

Launched applicant storytelling platform
Early adoption of video profiles

The platform successfully launched, and professionals created profiles and video introductions that demonstrated genuine interest in a richer approach to professional storytelling.

While adoption ultimately wasn't sufficient to justify the significant investment required to evolve it into a scalable commercial business, the experience provided valuable insight into both the promise of the concept and the realities of bringing a new product to market.

03

What I learned

Conviction must be paired with validation.

Bringing The Nutshells to life reinforced that great product ideas require more than conviction. They require the discipline to validate assumptions, learn from the market, and recognize when the timing or investment isn't yet right.
