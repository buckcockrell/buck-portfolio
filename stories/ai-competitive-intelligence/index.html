How do you use AI to move faster without sacrificing trust?

Building Trusted AI for Competitive Intelligence

Designed agentic AI workflows that helped Microsoft keep competitive battlecards current, accurate, and actionable across a rapidly evolving competitive landscape.

01

The challenge

Competitive intelligence is only valuable when it's current and trustworthy.

Competitive intelligence has always been one of product marketing's most valuable and time-intensive responsibilities. As Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365 Copilot faced rapidly evolving and increasingly intense competition, keeping battlecards current required continuously monitoring product announcements, documentation, blogs, analyst reports, and other public sources.

The challenge wasn't simply gathering information. It was ensuring every competitive comparison was complete, accurate, and defensible, reflecting Microsoft's high standards for factual claims and brand integrity.

Successful AI implementations aren't measured by how much work they automate. They're measured by how confidently organizations can rely on the results.

02

The approach

Use AI to augment expertise, not replace it.

Recognizing an opportunity to rethink the process, I developed a collection of agentic AI workflows that continuously gathered and analyzed publicly available information, compared competitor capabilities against Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365 Copilot, and surfaced meaningful competitive advantages for product marketing and field teams.

Rather than replacing human expertise, these workflows were intentionally designed to augment it, combining AI-driven research with structured validation and editorial review to ensure every recommendation met Microsoft's standards for accuracy and credibility before reaching the field.

The result transformed competitive intelligence from a highly manual research effort into a scalable, repeatable capability. The workflows compressed what had previously required tens of hours of research each week while providing sellers with more current, consistent battlecards that strengthened customer conversations and reinforced Microsoft's reputation for trustworthy competitive guidance.
