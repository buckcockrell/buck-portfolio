Story 09

How do you create one coherent digital experience from a collection of independent brands and business priorities?

Modernizing AOL's Corporate Web Strategy

Led the consolidation of AOL's corporate web properties into a unified digital experience, reducing complexity, strengthening the company's brand presence, and preparing for a major corporate milestone.

Company AOL
Focus Digital strategy and transformation
Capabilities
  • Digital Strategy
  • Information Architecture
  • Brand Strategy
  • Content Strategy
  • Stakeholder Management
  • Cross-functional Leadership
  • Web Governance
  • Change Management
01

The challenge

A fragmented web presence was creating complexity for the company and confusion for its audiences.

As AOL expanded through a series of acquisitions, its corporate web presence became increasingly fragmented. Individual business units maintained their own sites, creating inconsistent branding, duplicated content, and growing management overhead.

With the Verizon acquisition approaching, leadership recognized the need for a more unified corporate presence that clearly communicated the breadth of AOL's brands, advertising platforms, technologies, and business capabilities. I was asked to lead the effort.

Successful digital transformation isn't simply about redesigning websites. It's about creating clarity from complexity while bringing people together around a shared vision.

02

The approach

Bring disparate properties and independent teams together around a shared experience.

Working with developers, designers, brand managers, and stakeholders from across the organization, I led the information architecture and content strategy that brought these disparate properties together into a cohesive digital experience.

The work required balancing competing business priorities, preserving valuable brand equity, and creating a flexible structure that respected organizational ownership while reducing unnecessary duplication.

Equally important was building alignment across teams that had traditionally operated independently, ensuring the final experience reflected both the company's strategic direction and the needs of individual business groups.

The business result

A unified corporate presence delivered ahead of a major company milestone.

Unified corporate web presence
On schedule ahead of the Verizon acquisition announcement

Delivered under an aggressive timeline ahead of the Verizon acquisition announcement, the initiative established a modern, scalable foundation for AOL's corporate web presence.

By consolidating properties, implementing thoughtful redirects, and monitoring engagement throughout the transition, the team reduced management complexity while improving the experience for customers, partners, investors, prospective employees, and the media.

03

What I learned

Digital transformation is an exercise in organizational clarity.

The experience reinforced that successful digital transformation isn't simply about redesigning websites. It's about creating clarity from complexity while bringing people together around a shared vision.
