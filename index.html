A career in ten chapters

Every career tells a story.

These are the ten chapters that shaped mine.

Over more than two decades, I've worked across product marketing, go-to-market strategy, digital transformation, and AI, helping organizations launch products, navigate change, embrace new technologies, and create better customer experiences.

Looking back, I realized the projects I remember most weren't necessarily the biggest or the most visible. They were the ones that changed how I think about customers, products, leadership, and innovation.

The stories that follow aren't a complete career history. They're the experiences that continue to shape how I approach new challenges today.
